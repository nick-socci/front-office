# 0074. A count that ESPN's own matchup lines account for is explained without a register row

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-16
- Spec: [0147-espn-matchup-lines](../specs/0147-espn-matchup-lines/design.md) · Issue: #147

## Context

After ADR 0064, 74 past-season count values stay `unexplained`. Their sides have no
player-day difference behind them, or not enough. ESPN keeps a second record beside its
side total: each player's credited line for the matchup (`rosterForMatchupPeriod`). It is
already landed in every matchup-days capture and is not read (spec 0147, C1, C2). On all
43,906 count rows of eight seasons, our value less its game-line difference equals the
sum of those lines, except on #152's one side (C5). On 66 of the 74, the lines agree with
ours and ESPN's total does not (C6). 2026's register already records 8 such values by
hand as `espn_total_inconsistent`, from a spike capture of the same view. Seven of the
sides played a suspended game (C7); most of the rest are the season's last matchups (C8).

## Decision drivers

- The evidence is ESPN's own and landed, and the build can re-check it every time.
- The register must stay small enough to keep by hand (ADR 0064).
- A crediting rule the models lack must still fail. #152's start limit is the test case.

## Considered options

1. **A status from the lines**: a count whose value, less its game-line difference,
   equals ESPN's matchup lines, while ESPN's total differs from them, is
   `explained_by_matchup_lines`.
2. **Register rows generated by a script** from the same evidence.
3. **Register rows by hand**, as 2026's eight were.

## Decision

Chosen by the owner on 2026-10-16: **option 1**, decided after `registered` and `explained_by_game_lines`, so a
register row always wins. A side with lines takes a stat absent from them as 0; a side
with no lines is never explained this way. A rate's implied value shifts a component in
this status by our value less ESPN's. 2026's 31 register rows stay: they still match,
and they name their causes.

## Consequences

- Good: 66 counts, and the rates and results that follow them, are explained by a
  comparison the build repeats (spec 0147, C13).
- Good: #152's side stays `unexplained` (C4, C5). ESPN's lines apply the start limit, so
  ours does not equal them.
- Bad / accepted cost: the cause is not named. Suspended games (C7) and late-season
  totals (C8) are findings, not proven causes.
- Bad / accepted cost: where our crediting and ESPN's present lines agree with each other
  and differ from ESPN's total of the time (suspended games), the total of the time is
  not modelled.
- Follow-ups: from 2027, an in-season inconsistency is explained this way too, once its
  period has been captured after it ended.
